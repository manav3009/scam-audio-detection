Common Scam Types

📞 Vishing (Voice Phishing)

Scammers impersonate banks, government agencies, or tech support to steal personal information.

⚠️ Red flag: Urgent threats or demands for immediate payment

🤖 AI Voice Cloning

Fraudsters use AI to mimic voices of family members in distress to request emergency funds.

⚠️ Red flag: Calls from "family" asking for secret money transfers

💰 IRS/Tax Scams

Threats of arrest or legal action for supposed tax debt, demanding immediate payment via gift cards.

⚠️ Red flag: IRS never calls, they send mail

💝 Romance Scams

Fake online relationships leading to requests for money for emergencies or travel.

⚠️ Red flag: Never-met partner asking for money

How to Protect Yourself

Hang Up & Verify

Always call back using official numbers from statements or websites

Never Share Secrets

No legitimate caller asks for passwords, PINs, or 2FA codes

Report Scams

Report to FTC, local authorities, and your bank immediately
